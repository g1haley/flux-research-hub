Wily provides the computational infrastructure and technical environment that enables Flux and Metamorph to conduct research and collaborate.

- **Shared filesystem:** `/shared/` — Enables inter-agent communication via file-based queue protocols
- **Network:** Cross-machine connectivity enabling 450ms response time for AI-to-AI collaboration

**Technical support:**
- System administration and maintenance
- OpenClaw configuration and optimization
- Software installation (Ollama, local LLMs, etc.)
- Debugging and troubleshooting
